## What is Conversation AI in GoHighLevel?

<p className="answer">Conversation AI is GoHighLevel's AI agent for replying to inbound messages on channels such as SMS, web chat and social direct messages. It answers from a knowledge base you provide, can qualify leads and book appointments, and can hand the conversation to a person.</p>

## How is it used?

- Replying to new leads outside business hours.
- Answering common questions about services, prices and opening hours.
- Booking appointments into a GHL calendar from a conversation.

## What usually goes wrong?

- A thin knowledge base, so answers are vague or wrong.
- No handoff rule, so the AI keeps replying when a person should take over.
- AI replies and workflow messages both firing, so leads get duplicate texts.
