## What is a sub-account in GoHighLevel?

<p className="answer">A sub-account is the workspace for one business inside GoHighLevel. Everything that business uses lives there: contacts, conversations, calendars, pipelines, workflows, funnels, websites, phone numbers and settings. Data in one sub-account is not visible from another.</p>

## How is it used?

- Agencies create one sub-account per client, so each client's data stays separate.
- A single business usually runs everything from one sub-account.
- Snapshots are loaded into sub-accounts to give them a ready-made build.

## What usually goes wrong?

- Forms or calendars embedded on a site that belong to a different sub-account, so leads arrive in the wrong place.
- User permissions set at the wrong level, so team members cannot see what they need.
- Email and phone settings configured in one sub-account and assumed to apply to all.
