## What are opportunities and pipelines in GoHighLevel?

<p className="answer">An opportunity is a potential sale linked to a contact, with a value, a status and a stage. A pipeline is the ordered set of stages an opportunity moves through, such as New lead, Booked, Proposal and Won. A sub-account can have several pipelines, and workflows can create and move opportunities automatically.</p>

## How is it used?

- Seeing every open deal and where it stands on one board.
- Moving a lead to Booked automatically when they schedule a call.
- Reporting on how many leads reach each stage.

## What usually goes wrong?

- Stages moved by hand and forgotten, so the board stops reflecting reality.
- Several opportunities created for the same contact by overlapping workflows.
- Pipelines copied from another business that do not match how you sell.
