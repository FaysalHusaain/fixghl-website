## What is LC Email in GoHighLevel?

<p className="answer">LC Email, short for LeadConnector Email, is the built-in email sending service in GoHighLevel. A sub-account can send from a shared domain without setup, or from a dedicated sending domain with its own SPF and DKIM records, which gives better control over deliverability.</p>

## How is it used?

- Sending workflow emails, campaigns and one-to-one replies from GHL.
- Sending from a subdomain such as mail.yourdomain.com to keep it separate from your inbox provider.
- Tracking opens, clicks, bounces and unsubscribes inside GHL.

## Common problems

Emails land in spam when the dedicated sending domain was never set up or verified. Publishing a second SPF record on the same host breaks SPF for that domain, because a domain can only have one. A missing DMARC record is another common gap, since Google and Yahoo now expect one from bulk senders.
