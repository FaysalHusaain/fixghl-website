## Is GoHighLevel legit or a scam?

GoHighLevel is a legitimate software company, not a scam. The company is HighLevel LLC, a subsidiary of GoHighLevel Inc., with its head office in Dallas, Texas. Prices are published on its website, plans run month to month with no long-term contract, and you can cancel from your dashboard at any time.

The "scam" question usually comes from two places. The first is the volume of enthusiastic reviews online: HighLevel runs a large affiliate program, and many of those reviews are written by affiliates. We are one too, which is why this page has a disclosure. The second is usage billing. Calls, texts, emails and some AI features cost extra on top of the plan, which surprises people who expected one flat price.

The strongest case for GoHighLevel is that one account can replace several subscriptions, and contacts and users are unlimited on every plan. For agencies, sub-accounts and snapshots make it practical to run many clients from one login. The month-to-month billing and free trial keep the risk of trying it low.

## What are the downsides of GoHighLevel?

The main downsides are the setup effort, the extra usage costs and the learning curve. A new account needs a sending domain, phone registration, pipelines and workflows before it does anything useful. Some individual tools are less deep than dedicated products, and HighLevel changes the interface often, so guides and videos date quickly.

- **Setup takes real work.** Email deliverability, A2P 10DLC registration for US texting and workflow logic all need to be right. Accounts that skip this step are the ones we are most often asked to fix.
- **Usage costs on top.** Phone numbers, SMS, calls, email sending and AI agents are paid from a prepaid wallet. Our [GoHighLevel pricing guide](/guides/how-much-does-gohighlevel-cost) lists the current rates.
- **Breadth over depth.** In our experience, a business that relies heavily on one specialist tool, such as an advanced email platform or a large ecommerce store, may find the GHL version more basic.
- **Frequent changes.** New features and redesigned screens arrive regularly. That is good for the product, but menus move and older tutorials stop matching what you see.
- **Support is for agency users.** HighLevel's support button only shows for agency admins and agency users. Sub-account users go through whoever manages their agency account.

## How do you decide if GoHighLevel is right for you?

Decide by listing what you need the system to do, checking which of your current tools GHL replaces, and testing one complete lead flow during the free trial. If you can capture a lead, follow up automatically and book the call inside the trial, the platform will very likely work for you.

1. **List the jobs.** Write down what you need: capture leads, follow up, book, take payment, send reviews.
2. **Add up current costs.** Note what you pay each month for the tools that do those jobs today.
3. **Start the trial** on the plan you expect to use.
4. **Build one flow end to end:** a form, a follow-up workflow, a calendar and a pipeline.
5. **Test it with your own details** before you send any real traffic to it.
6. **Decide before the trial ends.** Billing starts automatically when it does, unless you cancel.
