## What is GoHighLevel used for?

GoHighLevel is used as the system behind a business's sales and marketing: one account holds the CRM, funnels and websites, email and SMS, calendars, workflow automation, reviews and payments. What every use case shares is fast, automatic follow-up, with each step recorded on the same contact.

| Agencies | Client sub-accounts, snapshots, SaaS mode, reporting |
| Local businesses | Missed-call text-back, review requests, AI chat replies, payments |

## How do coaches and consultants use GoHighLevel?

Coaches and consultants mostly use GoHighLevel to book discovery calls, follow up with people who have not booked, sell programs through funnels, and deliver courses or communities. The value is that the booking page, the follow-up and the payment all sit on the same contact record, so nothing falls between tools.

### 1. Booking discovery calls with automatic reminders

A booking calendar takes the call, and a workflow sends confirmations and reminders by email and SMS, then follows up with people who did not show. We build this in our [workflow automation service](/services/automation).

### 2. Selling a program through a funnel and checkout

A sales page, an order form and payments through a connected provider such as Stripe or PayPal, with the buyer tagged and moved into onboarding automatically. See our [funnels and websites service](/services/funnels-websites).

### 3. Running a course or a paid community

HighLevel includes courses, communities and memberships, so the program can be delivered in the same account that sold it. Our [GHL setup service](/services/ghl-setup) configures these alongside the rest of the account.

### 4. Filling a webinar with a registration funnel

HighLevel lists webinar funnels among its tools: a registration page, reminders before the session and follow-up afterwards. We build these as part of [funnels and websites](/services/funnels-websites).

## How do agencies use GoHighLevel?

Agencies use GoHighLevel to run every client from one agency login, giving each client a separate sub-account. Snapshots copy a proven setup into each new client, SaaS mode lets an agency sell the platform under its own brand, and dashboards report results. It turns client delivery into a repeatable system.

### 5. One sub-account per client, built from a snapshot

Each client gets its own sub-account with separate contacts, calendars and workflows, and a [snapshot](/guides/gohighlevel-snapshots) copies the agency's standard build into it. We set this up through our [GHL setup service](/services/ghl-setup).

### 6. Reselling GoHighLevel as the agency's own software

On the $497 plan, SaaS mode lets an agency sell subscriptions under its own brand, create client accounts automatically and rebill usage with a markup. See our [SaaS mode and white-label service](/services/saas-white-label).

### 7. Moving clients off other platforms

Agencies bring clients over from ClickFunnels, Wix, Kajabi or HubSpot so that everything runs in one place. Our [migration service](/services/migrations) moves the funnels, contacts and automations without losing data.

### 8. Reporting results to clients

Custom dashboards and reporting show leads, bookings and pipeline value for each client, so the agency can show what its work produced. We build reporting into [GHL setups](/services/ghl-setup) and connect ad and payment data through [integrations](/services/integrations).

## How do local businesses use GoHighLevel?

Local businesses use GoHighLevel to answer every inquiry quickly and win more repeat work. The most common uses are texting back missed calls, replying to website chat and social messages from one inbox, asking happy customers for reviews, and taking payments. Most of it runs automatically once it is set up.

### 9. Texting back every missed call

Missed-call text-back sends an automatic text when a call goes unanswered, so the caller gets a reply instead of moving on to a competitor. We set this up in our [workflow automation service](/services/automation).

### 10. Asking every customer for a review

After a job is marked complete, a workflow sends a review request by text or email, and reputation management collects the responses in one place. This is part of our [workflow automation service](/services/automation).

### 11. Answering chat, texts and social messages from one inbox

Conversations brings SMS, email, calls and social messages into one inbox, and Conversation AI can reply to common questions and book appointments. We connect the channels through our [integrations service](/services/integrations).

### 12. Sending invoices and taking payments

Payments and invoicing let a business send an invoice or a payment link and take payment through a provider such as Stripe or Square. We connect the payment provider through our [integrations service](/services/integrations).

## What is GoHighLevel not used for?

GoHighLevel is not the right tool for running a large online store with complex inventory, for accounting, or for project management. It can take payments and sell products, but businesses whose main need is ecommerce or bookkeeping usually keep a dedicated tool for that and connect it to GHL.

## How do you start with one use case?

Start with the use case that loses you the most money today, build it end to end, and add the next one only when the first works. For most businesses that is speed of follow-up: a form or missed call that gets an instant reply and a booking link.

1. **Pick one use case** from the list above.
2. **List what it needs:** a form or calendar, a phone number, a sending domain and a workflow.
3. **Build it in one sub-account** and test it with your own email and phone number.
4. **Switch it on for real leads** and check the results after a week.
5. **Add the next use case** once the first runs without you watching it.
