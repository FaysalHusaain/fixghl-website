## Which is cheaper, GoHighLevel or ClickFunnels?

Both start at $97 a month, so the plan price alone does not decide it. GoHighLevel adds usage charges for SMS, calls, email and AI, which scale with how much you send. ClickFunnels plans set limits on contacts and domains. Compare your total monthly cost, including the tools each platform would replace, such as a separate CRM, SMS tool or booking app.

## When is GoHighLevel the better fit?

- You follow up with leads by SMS and phone as well as email.
- You sell through booked calls or appointments and need reminders and no-show follow-up.
- You manage several businesses or clients and want each in its own sub-account.
- You run an agency and want to resell the platform under your own brand.

## When is ClickFunnels the better fit?

- Your business is mainly funnels, checkout and digital products.
- You do not need SMS, calling or multiple sales pipelines.
- Your team already knows ClickFunnels and the funnels are performing well.
